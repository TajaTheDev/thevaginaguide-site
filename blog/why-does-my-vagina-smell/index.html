Why does my vagina smell? 7 smells and what they mean

By Taja, women's health educator · November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

A healthy vagina has a mild smell, often tangy or slightly sour, because good bacteria make lactic acid that keeps vaginal pH around 3.8 to 4.5. A strong fishy smell, especially after sex or your period, often points to BV. A rotten smell can mean a forgotten tampon. Any smell with pain, fever, itching or unusual discharge needs a provider.

First things first: your vagina is supposed to smell like something. Not flowers, not "fresh linen," and definitely not a fragrance aisle. Like a person. Here's how to read the smells, so you know what's normal and what's worth a call.

Why vaginas have a smell at all

A healthy vagina is home to Lactobacillus bacteria that make lactic acid. That keeps your vaginal pH between about 3.8 and 4.5, roughly as acidic as tomato juice, and it gives a mild tangy scent. Sweat glands on the vulva, your cycle, sex, food and hormones all shift the smell a little day to day.

Why the fishy smell gets worse after sex

Semen is alkaline. When it mixes with vaginal fluid, it raises pH and releases compounds called amines, the same reaction doctors use in a quick office check for BV (the whiff test). If you notice a fishy smell the day after sex or right after your period, that's a classic BV clue. (Keeps coming back? Read why BV keeps coming back.)

What makes odor worse (even though it's marketed to fix it)

  • Douching. It washes away protective bacteria and is linked with BV.
  • Scented washes, wipes and sprays. They can irritate the vulva and disrupt your pH.
  • Washing inside your vagina. It cleans itself. Warm water on the outside is enough.

What actually helps

  • Breathable cotton underwear, and change out of sweaty or wet clothes quickly.
  • Rinse the vulva with water. Use a gentle, unscented soap on the outside only if you like.
  • Change pads and tampons regularly.
  • Treat the cause. If it's BV or trich, no wash will fix it, but treatment will.

See a provider if

A smell is strong or fishy, it comes with itching, burning, pain, fever or unusual discharge, you're pregnant, or a smell doesn't go away after a few days.

Questions people ask

What should a healthy vagina smell like?

Usually mildly tangy or slightly sour, like yogurt, because healthy bacteria make lactic acid. It can shift slightly with your cycle, sweat and sex.

Why does my vagina smell like fish?

A fishy smell is a common sign of bacterial vaginosis or trichomoniasis, and it's often stronger after sex or your period. Both need prescription treatment, so see a provider.

Why does my vagina smell like metal?

A metallic smell usually comes from blood, such as during your period, spotting or light bleeding after sex. It's typically normal.

How do I get rid of vaginal odor fast?

Skip douching and scented products, rinse the outside with water, wear cotton and change sweaty clothes. A fishy or foul smell usually means an infection or a forgotten tampon, which needs treatment rather than washing.
