Privacy policy

This policy explains what information The Vagina Guide ("I", "me") collects through thevaginaguide.com and how it's used.

What I collect

  • Your email address when you sign up for a free guide, the blog or an app waitlist, plus any choices you make on the form (like which app you're interested in).
  • Purchase details when you buy a guide: your name, email and order. Payments are processed by Kit and Stripe; I never see or store your card number.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, collected through privacy-friendly analytics from my hosting provider.

How I use it

  • To send the guide you asked for and the emails you signed up for.
  • To deliver purchases and answer support requests.
  • To understand which content is helpful and improve the site.

I don't sell your personal information. I don't ask for or store your health symptoms.

Who helps run the site

  • Kit (email list, forms and checkout)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Cloudflare (hosting, security and analytics)

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can ask me to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@thevaginaguide.com.

Kids

This site is for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
