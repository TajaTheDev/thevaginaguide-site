Questions

When can I start?

Gentle breathing and light Kegels can usually start in the first days after a vaginal birth if they feel OK. Get your provider's OK first after a C-section. The guide tells you exactly what to do in each phase.

I'm a year (or five) postpartum. Is it too late?

Not at all. It's never too late to train your pelvic floor and core. Start at Phase 1 for a week, then move up.

I had a C-section. Is this for me?

Yes. Your pelvic floor still carried your pregnancy for 9 months. There's a full section on C-section recovery and scar care.

How much time does it take?

About 10 minutes a day. Kegels can be done during feeds or diaper changes.

No. It's education to help you understand your body and have better conversations with your provider. It can't diagnose or treat you.
