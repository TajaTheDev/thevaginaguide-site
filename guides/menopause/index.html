Questions

I'm in perimenopause. Is this for me?

Yes. Dryness, urgency and leaks can start in perimenopause. Everything in this guide applies.

Is vaginal estrogen safe?

The guide explains what the research and The Menopause Society say, including who should talk to their oncologist first. Your doctor can tell you what's right for you.

I'm in my 60s or 70s. Will the exercises still work?

Yes. Your pelvic floor is muscle, and muscle responds to training at any age. The exercises start gentle and lying down.
