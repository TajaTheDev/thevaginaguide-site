Questions

When can I start after a C-section?

Walking and gentle breathing usually start in the hospital. The guide walks you through each week, and core work starts once your provider clears you, often around 6 weeks.

When can I start scar massage?

Only once the incision is fully closed and your provider says it's OK, often around 6 to 8 weeks. The guide has the full step-by-step.

Will the "shelf" above my scar go away?

Swelling, loose skin and scar tissue all play a part. Time, scar massage and deep core work often help, and the guide explains when to get it checked.

I had a vaginal birth. Is this the same guide?

Yes. It's the full Postpartum Repair guide, with recovery plans for both vaginal and C-section births.
